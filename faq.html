Popular travel questions

Answers to the questions luxury travelers ask most

How quickly can I receive a bespoke itinerary?

Once you share your travel dates and interests, our consultants typically deliver a refined itinerary within 24 to 48 hours for review and adjustment.

Can Kaya arrange private vehicles and guides?

Absolutely. We arrange private 4x4 safari vehicles, expert guides and luxury transfers so your group travels in comfort and privacy.

Will my safari be adapted to my pace?

Yes. We build each itinerary around your preferred balance of wildlife viewing, cultural immersion, relaxation and wellness.

What if my international flights change?

Notify our team and we will update your airport transfers, local flights and arrival schedule to keep your journey seamless.
