Common Questions About Booking

Find quick answers to frequently asked questions. Don't see yours? Contact us directly—we're always happy to help.

We recommend booking 2-3 months in advance for the best availability and pricing. However, we can arrange last-minute safaris (as early as 2 weeks) subject to availability. Peak seasons (July-October) book faster, so early planning is ideal for securing premium lodges.

We accept bank transfers, credit cards (Visa/Mastercard), mobile money (M-Pesa), PayPal, and cryptocurrency. A 30% deposit secures your booking, with final payment due 30 days before departure. We offer flexible payment plans for group bookings.

Visa requirements depend on your nationality. Most visitors can obtain an e-visa online at evisa.go.ke or on arrival at the airport. We recommend checking with your embassy, and we're happy to provide guidance on travel documentation requirements for your specific country.

Yellow fever vaccination is recommended for most travelers to Kenya. We advise consulting your doctor about vaccinations appropriate for your health profile. Malaria prophylaxis is recommended for certain areas and seasons. Check requirements 4-6 weeks before travel.

Yes, we offer flexible changes up to 30 days before your safari. Cancellations more than 60 days prior receive a full refund. For cancellations within 60 days, fees apply based on our standard policy (starting at 25%). We strongly recommend travel insurance for additional coverage.
