Our Story

Kaya Experience is born from East African soul

We believe the most memorable journeys are shaped by people, place and purpose. Kaya Experience Limited was founded to take guests beyond the ordinary, where every itinerary is emotionally layered and every moment feels authentic.

From fishermen on the coast to bodaboda riders in Nairobi, from mama mboga market storytellers to Maasai elders in the Mara, our experiences are built around the people who make East Africa alive.

  • Premium safari and coastal journeys with local authenticity
  • Emotion-driven travel design for couples, families and groups
  • Luxury comfort balanced with meaningful East African connection
