Tour Overview

Step into the heartbeat of East Africa with a curated safari that blends the golden plains of the Maasai Mara, private wildlife encounters, and authentic local hospitality. This journey is crafted for travellers who seek both exhilarating wildlife spectacle and intimate, luxurious comfort.

Each morning begins with dawn game drives as the Mara awakens, while evenings unfold around elegant campfires beneath endless skies. Enjoy tailored experiences from exclusive sundowners to meaningful Maasai village visits — all designed to create unforgettable memories.

Key Tour Highlights

  • Private sunrise game drives in Maasai Mara
  • Luxury tented camp with panoramic views
  • Authentic Maasai cultural visits
  • Gourmet meals and premium beverages
  • Expert local guide & private safari vehicle
  • Access to exclusive photographic vantage points
  • Luxury transfers and airport assistance
  • Small group service for calm, curated travel

Itinerary Timeline

Day 1 — Arrival and Mara Sunrise

Arrive at Nairobi and connect to the Mara. Settle in, enjoy a welcome lunch, then take your first private game drive as the golden light reveals grazing wildlife.

  • Luxury safari camp transfer
  • Afternoon game drive
  • Welcome dinner under the stars

Day 2 — Big Five Game Drive

Spend the day on a full safari circuit, tracking the Big Five with your expert guide. Enjoy a premium picnic in a secluded spot and return as the sunset paints the savannah.

  • Morning and evening game drives
  • Luxury picnic experience
  • Private safari vehicle and guide

Day 3 — Maasai Culture & Sundowners

Visit a Maasai village to learn local customs and storytelling. Afternoon game drive followed by a signature sundowner with panoramic views over the plains.

  • Maasai village cultural immersion
  • Traditional refreshments
  • Sunset drinks at a private viewpoint

Day 4 — Full Day Mara Exploration

Explore the Mara’s varied landscapes with an extended game drive and optional photography session. Enjoy an evening feast and campfire stories.

  • Full-day private game drive
  • Picnic lunch in the bush
  • Evening campfire experience

Day 5 — Departure via Nairobi

Enjoy a final morning drive before returning to Nairobi. Transfer to the airport with refined memories and a dedicated departure service.

  • Morning transfer to airstrip
  • Flight to Nairobi
  • Airport drop-off or onward assistance
